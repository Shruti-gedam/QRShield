      About QRShield
      Context-Aware QR Payment Fraud Detection Framework
      
    
      
      
      
        
      QRShield is a rule-based heuristic analysis framework that inspects QR codes for payment fraud and deception. Five independent modules — payload parsing, URL intelligence, context analysis, NLP deception detection, and risk scoring — produce a transparent, explainable risk score with actionable recommendations.
      
        
      
          
      📷
      QR Decoder
      pyzbar primary + OpenCV adaptive-threshold fallback
      
          
      📦
      Payload Parser
      UPI, HTTPS, deep-links, vCard, WiFi, Geo, plain text
      
          
      🌐
      URL Analyzer
      Scheme, homoglyphs, redirect chain, DNS, Safe Browsing, VirusTotal
      
          
      🕐
      Context Engine
      Temporal, geo, device, network, velocity, VPN/Tor detection
      
          
      💬
      Deception Detector
      NLP intent extraction, pay/receive mismatch, urgency, authority spoofing
      
          
      🎯
      Risk Engine
      Aggregates all signals into a 0-100 score + verdict + recommendations
      
        
      
        
      
          Rule-based · No ML · Fully transparent · v1.0.0
